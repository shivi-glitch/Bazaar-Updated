## 4. How news and price relate (the one rule behind every beat)

- **Round 1:** an event starts moving the price at `t_E` and the headline appears at the same instant for everyone. The move itself is always **spread over many ticks** (8–55 s, shape per 04 §3), never a jump. A fast reader joins mid-move; a slow reader sees most of it done.
- **Round 2:** the same event starts moving the (reactive) market at `t_E`; a headline reaches a team `15 / 10 / 7 / 3` seconds later by Research level. A level-0 team receives the news when roughly half to most of the move has happened (02 §6).
- A headline is only as good as its source. The same event can have several signals; some are right, some wrong.

## 5. Session-by-session narrative

Net session returns (binding targets) are in `03` §2. Event IDs refer to `02` §2; exact headline texts and release times are in `02` §4.

### R1S1 — "Opening Bell" · calm · regime ×0.8 · 7 instruments
- **World:** stable growth, cheap credit, MCB holds rates. GOLDCORE barely moves, PULSE is quiet.
- **Beats:** E1.1 (t=120) MCB holds rates — priced in, HDFC-W dips 0.4% (newspaper implies up: wrong). E1.2 (t=200) a finfluencer pumps BZR-V: spike +2.5% then fades to −1.5% net.
- **Behaviour:** INFY-Z (+3.0) and HDFC-W (+2.5) trend. TATA-Y swings ±1.8% around its average (net +0.5), the first planted edge. BZR-V swings with no net direction.
- **Lesson:** read the chart, fees punish churn, finfluencers are unreliable.
- **Winning play:** buy trend, trade TATA-Y's swings with SMA20, ignore the BZR-V pump.

### R1S2 — "Cheap Money" · euphoric growth names · ×0.9 · NIMBUS unlocks
- **World:** loan growth accelerates; PULSE catches its own speculative rally.
- **Beats:** E2.1 (t=60) record loan growth → HDFC-W +2.0. **E2.2 (t=110)** Infinity logistics contract: INFY-Z +2.5 then a further +2.0 drift over ~40 s (post-news drift edge); a finfluencer says INFY-Z is "done" (wrong). E2.4 (t=180) PULSE rally +5 over 40 s (the finfluencer is right). E2.5 (t=230) Nimbus earnings beat: +3.5 then +2.0 drift.
- **Lesson:** tech names move together; the move often continues after the headline; analyst > finfluencer.
- **Winning play:** buy INFY-Z/NIMBUS on the news and hold the drift; take PULSE profits.

### R1S3 — "A Small Cough" · calm · ×0.9 · STAPLE unlocks

### R1S4 — "The Corridor Closes" (THE WAR) · ×1.3 · VOLTAGE unlocks

## 7. Fundamentals narrative rule
EPS (reported at each session open) leads price direction: `growth ≈ 0.8 × session return + noise (σ 1.5)` so careful readers get a ~75–80% hint. Exceptions: **HDFC-W** and **APEX** (headline EPS looks fine until the disclosure; the *Gross NPA* chart is the real clue). Numbers are in `03` §4–§7.
